2026-2027 Season

BIOBUZZ™

Welcome to the FIRST® CANOPY™ season! This year, we're diving into BIOBUZZ™ presented by RTX, a challenge that blends engineering excellence with the vibrant energy of the natural world.

The Mission

Teams will use their engineering skills to amplify the excitement and energy found within nature, designing robots that can interact with the environment in innovative ways.

Game Element: Pollen

The primary scoring element this season is Pollen—plastic balls approximately 3 inches in diameter. Efficiency in handling and scoring Pollen will be key to victory.

Key Date

The full game reveal and competition manual were released on September 12, 2026. We are already hard at work designing our approach!
