What we're all about

Who we are

We're FTC Team 31742, SuperNOVA. We're a group of middle schoolers from Bear Valley Middle School who spend way too much time thinking about gears, code, and how to make a robot actually do what we want.

We're lucky enough to be mentored by the high schoolers from FRC Team 3255 (the SuperNURDs) at San Pasqual. They've taught us everything from how to use a drill without breaking things to the basics of Java.

Why we do it

Honestly? Because building things is awesome. We love the challenge of the FIRST Tech Challenge—taking a weird seasonal prompt and trying to solve it with a pile of aluminum and some clever code.

Between the late-night build sessions and the stress of competition day, we're learning how to actually work as a team and solve problems that don't have an answer in the back of a textbook.

The FTC Game

If you're not familiar, FTC is basically a giant puzzle for students in grades 6-12. Every year, we get a new game, and we have to design, build, and program a robot in just 12 weeks. It's not just about the robot, though—we also keep an engineering notebook and do outreach in our community.

Home Base

We're based in Escondido, CA. Having a partnership with the SuperNURDs gives us access to a real workshop and mentors who have been doing this for years, which lets us compete with the best teams in San Diego.
